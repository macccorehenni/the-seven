
# The description o
“Someone’s following us.”

Flynn was the first to feel it — a tug in the pattern, a ripple where there should have been stillness. They and the rest of the Seven were still an hour or so from Aspen and Atero’s last known location, but the pull was strong enough that even at this distance, the fox and the ōkami brushed against their senses.

  

Amaria felt it too. Her eyes flicked toward Flynn, a quiet confirmation passing between them.

  

“Whoever they are,” she murmured, “they aren’t… hostile.”

  

Flynn nodded, mantle dimming and brightening in a thoughtful pulse.

“That much I know. They feel like… they want to help us. Should we tell Aunt Armani?”

  

Amaria shook her head, conviction settling over her like a second skin.

“Not yet. Let them get closer. Their resonance is too large for one person — there are two of them, and neither feels like a threat.”

  

Scout’s tail twitched. “Who do you think they are? Another trick from Lyria’s uncle? Or do they really want to help?”

  

Amaria’s eyes glossed over, her breath catching as her sight shifted. Tansei moved closer on instinct, steadying her as the vision sharpened: Atero moving through the forest with fox‑stillness and intent, Aspen trailing behind her, fussing over plants he absolutely did not need to be touching.

  

“There are two,” Amaria whispered. “One wants to protect Lyria. I don’t know why. The other… wants to protect them.”
