
# Prolouge— A mysterious figure
The castle was asleep, something which was rare to behold. Its walls were quiet, satisfaction at the day bleeding into comfort at night. The sigils which lined its walls, which gave it powers only those who knew the queen would understand, were quietly glowing. 

Its matriarchs, Liora the Dawn-bringer, and Queen Armani, the Dusk-bringer, slept peacefully, normally the busiest women in Vulpinterra, as were the Seven who surrounded them. 

Scout, normally a kitsune who never stood still, lay sprawled out across Kalalia, whose body still hummed with the sort of energy chasing after him would bring. 

Azul and Tol slept soundly to the right of the two kitsune with their cyberwolf tails, metal and fur, entangled in a sign of trust. They had found sleep tired from a day of work both at the College and in the workshop Azul had made with her bare hands. 

Ina, her friend Tie, and her two guardians, phoenix of guardianship Dawnbreak and dryad of decay Thaylena, slept nearby. Their resonance synched to each other, protecting the ones they loved, whimsy lacing with loyalty to them even in sleep. 

Somaria slept too, her partner for life Tanilia sleeping up against her side. Their tanuki tails and elven ears twitched in dreams only they could see. 

Flynn was sitting against the door of their sleeping room, a book discarded on their lap. His girl Kifi slept with him, mouth still twitching after shifting from her stone form while play fighting with Flynn and Scout earlier in the day. 

But all was not well for the youngest of the group. 

Lyria stood apart from her family, awake, underneath a shadow not found in the castle during the day. She paced around the room, stealing glances at Dawnbreak, peacefully sleeping, not aware of the inner turmoil its younger bonded was feeling as she rubbed her left arm where it had left its mark. 

The mark was dim, not in exhaustion, but in anticipation of something dreadful, something from her past, from when she was but a shadow, at the whims of her father, who wanted nothing more to puppet her, to shape her into his slave. 

Someone wanted that once again, and Lyria could feel it. 

All the lights flickered off, and a mysterious yet familiar figure appeared, its body obscured in the shadows that didn't belong. 

"You've grown, child."

The voice chilled Lyria. It sounded like her father's, which she had come to terms with hearing after he made up for what he had done. But this voice didn't sound like his. It sounded cruder, almost as if hewn out of the stone surrounding Duskterra, not like Lyria's voice, a child of becoming, like bells ringing. 

But tonight, her voice was fragile, like she was once again a shadow. In this figures presence, she felt weak. She mentally cried out for help, but nobody could hear her. She felt this in her body, and her resonance felt off. The whole evening into the night, she had felt hopeless… something she hadn't felt for over a decade as she grew with her found family. 

She shuddered as the shadow moved closer to her. 

"Your father was weak. I'm here to make you into what you _should have_ become."

Finally, Lyria regained some of her composure. 

"Uncle Ampor. How… nice to see you."

He moved closer, and all composure that Lyria had vanished in that moment. Pure terror came across her as she ran. Ampor stayed up close to her the whole time. 

"You cannot run from what has always been in you, Ly-ri-a, child."

Lyria ran, her body carrying her on sheer will, until she makes a wrong turn, and ends up at a dead end, the corridor that normally felt like it belonged to all, its usamagi energy dimmed to a simmer as it slept. Amaria and Tansei slept in a room on this corridor, unaware of their bondmate's troubles. She shouted for them, terror splayed across her face, until she felt the weight of her uncle lean across her face. 

No one responded.

But she ran. And ran. But her body gave out to darkness. She fell, right into her uncle's arms. 

"You can't run forever, child."

That was the last thing she remembered before the trees of the ōkami lands— corrupted by pain and shadow— became visible to her, as did a cage, built of her own soul. 


She fainted from desperation, believing this was all in the Dream Kingdom, was all something her twisted mind wanted. But it was real. She was trapped again. 
# Chapter 1— The Castle awakens

Scout yawned as he awoke, nonchalant as usual. His ears pricked up as he checked the room. He realized, terror filling his body, that there was a hole in his resonance. 

Instinctively he ran to Flynn and shook them awake. Flynn, used to being shaked awake, mumbled into Kifi's side, "Scout—"

But then they looked up and saw their brother with an emotion he never carried. Their instincts flared, realizing themselves that they knew exactly why the castle didn't feel like home—

"She's missing." Flynn panicked, jolting Kifi awake enough she shifted into her guard-stone form. 

"What's going on? Is there a problem?"

Kifi stood, or tried to anyways. Her stone-guardian transformation was still new to her, and she stumbled back down, the flagstone of the room scraping her knees. She growled loud to make up for her lack of mobility, frustrated she could not protect her partner.

Flynn stood, their mantle flickering with the loss of a piece of their stability, as they absentmindedly helped Kifi up. Flynn was a kitsune who _thrived_ on patterns. Having one piece of his family inexplicably missing bothered him, and Scout panicking didn't help either. It hit them like a rock thrown in a pond. They didn't react at first, absorbed in their own thought process. But recognition skewed their face as they realized something was missing. They ran to the wall to observe the sigils, as Kifi awkwardly followed them, Scout uncharacteristically following a good distance behind them. 

"They look weirdly fine." Flynn frowned. 

Kifi, who had shifted her form back to human, sighed. "At least we can all agree that she is not here, right?"

Scout sighed dramatically. "What will we do without her?"

Flynn, perhaps forgetting their brother was a magnet for drama, said, "Well, we'll find her."

Azul joined the three, as did Kalilia. 

"Those sigils are a bit dim, though." Azul frowned.
They left the room, compelled by Flynn's movement. The rest of the group followed them, slowly attracting the other members. They eventually ended up at the hallway where Amaraia and Tansei sat outside their quiet room. 

Flynn paused, deep in thought. "Did you guys hear anything out here overnight?"
